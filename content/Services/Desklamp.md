> Remember everything you read
> Engage more with your reading, organize your thoughts, and find ideas faster.

[Desklamp](https://desklamp.io/) is a tool designed for students, professors, and researchers to enhance their digital reading experience. It offers features such as PDF annotation, collaborative tools, and organizational tools. It offers light / dark mode along with syncing to cloud services.

What I enjoy most about this is the collaborative aspect, where you have the ability to read and write with other people in real-time.
