The [Remarkable Tablet](https://remarkable.com) is an E-ink device specifically designed for productivity and getting things done. It's been designed to be non-distracting, and intentionally excludes any sort of app-store or repository. Because of this, the Remarkable Tablet is a phenomenal option for writing as it provides a feeling that some describe better than paper. The Remarkable Tablet allows you to sync PDFs and other documents from your Remarkable to your phone or PC. Because of this, reading books from your device is easy as you simply upload the document on the app. Additionally, there are scripts which can enhance the device, such as creating a "daily briefing" from an RSS feed, essentially taking a snapshot of the feed every morning, and converting it to a PDF for you to view on your device- all automatically.

This tablet is extremely beneficial for daily journaling as it's completely distraction-free allowing you to get in a [[Flow State]] while writing. While not cheap, it certainty has many advantages compared to other E-ink devices, and if your number one goal is productivity, then this is absolutely the device for you.
