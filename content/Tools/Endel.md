[Endel](https://endel.io) offers personalized soundscapes to help you focus, relax, and sleep. Backed by neuroscience. Endel is able to analyze real-time data such as weather, heart rate, sunrise / sunset and reacts to that, adapting the music so it is specially tuned for the listener. Backed by neuroscientists, they have found a 7x increase in focus in one [study](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.04.02.438269v3.full.pdf) 

Additionally, Endel is able to work on Iphone, Android, Apple Watch, Android watches and even on some smart TVs. Endel offers an "autorun" feature, where it is able to automatically change each type of music you are listening to throughout the day, for instance in the morning it will start a playlist specially designed for "morning focus", and as the day starts to wind down, will play relaxing music and eventually sleep music towards the night around your scheduled sleep time.

For more information on how Endel works, visit their [science page](https://endel.io/science).
