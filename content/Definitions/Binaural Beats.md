>[ Binaural beats](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binaural_beats) are an auditory illusion created when two different pure-tone sinewaves are presented to each ear. For instance, if 530 Hz is played in one ear, and 520 Hz is played in the other, the brain perceives a tone at 10Hz. This type of behavior can be used to manipulate the brain for purposes such as sleep, relaxation, etc.

A [study](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binaural_beats) conducted in 2023, determined that binaural beats actually _deteriorated_ performance during an intelligence test. There is not a consensus within the scientific community on the effectiveness of binaural beats. Binaural beats may be effective to some, but not for everyone. It should also be noted that Binaural Beats may need stereo headphones to function properly.
