For music that is specially designed for improving focus & productivity, below is a curated list of services which are specially designed, alternative to [[Binaural Beats]].
- [Brain.fm ](https://www.brain.fm/) - Music Powered By Science
	- Brain.fm has conducted a [study](https://rdcu.be/d69Hg) where participants listened to Brain.fm along with other types of music, determined from FMRIs and EEGs during the session, that brain activity & blood flow actually increased while listening to Brain.fm, meaning that the brain was more active and functional. *It is important to note that Brain.fm is NOT binaural beats*. For additional information, read the [Brain.fm white paper](https://www.brain.fm/pdfs/white-paper.pdf).
